MARVELLOUS TECH SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
Employee Handbook & HR Policy Document
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

---

SECTION 1: LEAVE POLICY

Employees are entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Annual leave accrues at the rate of 2 days per month of service. Unused
leave can be carried forward to the next year, up to a maximum of 10 days.
Leave requests must be submitted at least 3 working days in advance through
the HR portal, except in emergencies.

Sick leave is granted separately, up to 12 days per year, and does not
require advance notice but must be reported to the reporting manager on
the same day.

Maternity leave is 26 weeks as per statutory requirements. Paternity leave
is 2 weeks, to be availed within 6 months of the child's birth.

---

SECTION 2: WORK FROM HOME POLICY

Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with prior approval
from their reporting manager. Engineering and QA teams on active sprint
deadlines may be asked to work from office during critical release weeks.

All remote employees must be available on Slack and video calls during
core hours: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST.

---

SECTION 3: PROBATION PERIOD

All new employees undergo a probation period of 3 months from their date
of joining. During probation, employees are eligible for all standard
benefits except annual leave carry-forward, which only applies after
confirmation.

Performance review at the end of probation determines confirmation. HR
and the reporting manager jointly evaluate based on a standardized scorecard
covering technical delivery, collaboration, and adherence to timelines.

---

SECTION 4: REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

Employees can claim reimbursement for internet bills up to Rs. 1,500 per
month when working from home, and mobile bills up to Rs. 1,000 per month
for employees in client-facing or on-call roles.

Travel reimbursement for client visits covers economy class flights, AC
train travel, or standard cab fare, with prior manager approval required
for expenses above Rs. 5,000.

Reimbursement claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense,
along with valid bills, through the Finance portal.

---

SECTION 5: CODE OF CONDUCT

Employees are expected to maintain professionalism in all official
communication. Harassment of any kind, including verbal, physical, or
online, is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate disciplinary
action, up to and including termination.

Conflicts of interest, including outside employment that competes with
company business, must be disclosed to HR in writing.

---

SECTION 6: RESIGNATION AND NOTICE PERIOD

The standard notice period is 60 days for confirmed employees and 15 days
for employees on probation. Employees may buy out the remaining notice
period subject to manager and HR approval, at a rate of one month's gross
salary per month of notice bought out.

Exit interviews are mandatory and are scheduled by HR within the final
week of the notice period. Full and final settlement is processed within
45 days of the last working day.

---

SECTION 7: PERFORMANCE APPRAISAL

Performance appraisals are conducted twice a year, in April and October.
Appraisals are based on a combination of self-assessment, manager review,
and peer feedback (360-degree review) for employees at Senior Engineer
level and above.

Salary revisions, where applicable, are typically effective from the
month following the appraisal cycle, subject to business approval.
